Frequently Asked Questions

Is Free Up My Mac really free?

Yes, completely free. No subscriptions, no in-app purchases, no hidden costs.

Is my data safe?

Absolutely. The app runs entirely on your Mac - no data is sent to any servers. Files are moved to Trash (not permanently deleted), so you can recover them anytime.

What files does it protect?

The app automatically skips system files, hidden files, app bundles, and critical folders like /System and /Library. Only your personal duplicate files are identified.

What are the system requirements?

macOS 13 (Ventura) or later. Works on all Macs with Apple Silicon or Intel processors.

How does it detect duplicates?

Using content-based detection with xxHash64 algorithm. Files are identified as duplicates only if their contents are identical - not by filename or date.
